About me

It all started with my love for Roblox and LEGO. I’ve always liked building things, so about two years ago I started scripting my own stuff in Roblox and just kept going. These days I’m comfortable with the advanced stuff: modules, typed Luau, keeping everything important on the server, and working out what could go wrong before players find it.

I’d rather take a bit longer and do it right than rush something that breaks the moment you add a new feature.

Beyond Luau

Besides Luau, I know C++ at a beginner level, and I’m interested in cybersecurity and making games in Godot.

I also love Arduino and electronics. I enjoy repairing computers and phones too, and I do that for people as well.
